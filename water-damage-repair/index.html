Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Ventilate with the bathroom exhaust fan while showering and afterward to protect new drywall.
  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Fresh caulk every year around showers, tubs and sinks stops water reaching the wall cavity.
  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.

When to Call for Water Damage Repair in Columbia

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • Wood floor boards stayed raised, cupped or crowned even once the moisture was gone.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.
  • Wall cavities remain open where drywall was removed for drying.
